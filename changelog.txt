Version: 0.11.0
Date: 2026-10-07
  Changes:
    - The gamble is its own window again, about as wide as a chest window: your inventory on the
      left, the wheel of fortune on the right, spin button at the bottom. E and Esc close it, and
      it remembers where you dragged it.
    - The inventory in the window moves your real items with the usual controls: left click picks
      up (the slot is marked with the hand, Q puts it back), places or swaps, right click takes
      half or places one item. Shift click moves a stack into the stake slot (shift + right click
      half of it), Ctrl + click collects all items of that kind into the stake slot.
    - Esc closes the target selection first, like vanilla popups, before the window itself.

Version: 0.10.0
Date: 2026-10-07
  Changes:
    - ALT + G opens the normal character window with the wheel of fortune attached to the right,
      like a building. The odd empty box next to the inventory is gone.
    - The stake slot is a supply now: a slider and amount field next to it set how much is staked

Version: 0.9.0
Date: 2026-10-07
  Features:
    - Prize slot: wins land there instead of going straight into the inventory. Closing the window
      moves uncollected prizes into your inventory, whatever does not fit drops to the ground.
      Like a machine with a full output, the reel will not spin while a new prize would not fit.
  Changes:
    - The stake slot now sits in the gamble panel next to the target and the prize slot instead of
      inside the inventory window. Stake and prize slots behave like vanilla slots: left click to
      put in, take out or swap, right click to put in one item or take half, shift click to move
      to the inventory.
    - Larger spacing on the reel: win fields are further apart (every 3rd to 6th field) and there
      are small gaps between fields.
    - Near misses are rarer (about one spin in five) and less extreme; only very close ones hang
      on the edge for a moment.
  Bugfixes:
    - The reel clicks from the very first moment of a spin again, rattling while it rushes by.

Version: 0.7.0
Date: 2026-10-07
  Features:
    - Spins vary in length. Short, brisk spins are almost always a miss. Long spins crawl slowly
      at the end so every click is audible - usually a win, sometimes a painful near miss.
    - Near misses: the reel hangs on the edge of a win field for a moment and then tips back onto
      the miss, or slips off a win field it had just reached.
    - Close wins: after a long spin the win field creeps over the edge, hangs there briefly and
      then clicks into place.
    - No extra effects; the tension comes only from speed, pauses and the clicks.

Version: 0.6.0
Date: 2026-10-07
  Changes:
    - The window works like a chest now: the stake is a real slot next to the vanilla inventory.
      Left click, right click, shift click and splitting stacks all work as everywhere else. The
      gamble panel is attached to the right of it. The home-made inventory grid is gone.
    - Target selection works like the constant combinator: click the target slot, pick an item
      from the item group tabs, choose quality and amount, then confirm (double click or Enter
      confirm too).
    - The reel always comes to rest exactly in the middle of a field. It stops slightly off, then
      settles into the centre of the nearest field.
    - Win fields on the reel are evenly spaced (every 2nd to 5th field, depending on the chance).
  Bugfixes:
    - With time paused (e.g. in the map editor) the reel no longer freezes the window; the spin is
      resolved immediately instead, since nothing can move without ticks.
    - The reel counts its own frames, so a save loaded mid-spin carries on where it left off.

Version: 0.5.0
Date: 2026-10-07
  Features:
    - Reel animation. A row of fields rushes sideways and slows down until the result stops under
      the arrow. Win fields show the target item in yellow, misses are empty. The reel moves pixel
      by pixel, clicks softly as fields pass the arrow and plays a fanfare on a win.
    - The result is decided before the reel starts; the reel only shows it. The prize is paid out
      when the reel stops, so the inventory in the window does not give it away early.
    - The spin state lives in the save, so saving mid-spin and multiplayer are safe.
  Changes:
    - While the reel spins, stake, target and the spin button are locked.
    - Closing the window mid-spin pays out at once and posts the result in chat.

Version: 0.4.0
Date: 2026-10-07
  Changes:
    - One window instead of two. Your inventory is shown inside the gamble window, so there is no
      need to open the character screen and pick up a stack first. E and Esc close the window.
    - Staking works like everywhere else in the game: left click an inventory slot stakes the whole
      stack, right click stakes a single item. On the stake slot, left click takes everything back
      and right click takes one item back.
    - Staked items stay in your inventory until you spin. The grid shows what is left and
      highlights the slots involved. Spinning takes from the clicked slots first; freshness is the
      average of exactly the items that are taken.
    - Items without a value and armor with equipment inside are greyed out in the grid, the
      tooltip says why.
  Bugfixes:
    - Items still lying in the stake slot of 0.3.0 are given back when the save is loaded.

Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-10-07
  Features:
    - Later items are worth more. Value = material value x (1 + weight x log10(1 + research time /
      1000 s)), counting all research up to the recipe, its ingredients and the building it needs.
    - Map setting "Value factor for game progress" (default 1.5, 0 turns it off). Changing it
      recalculates all values right away.
  Changes:
    - Because of the progress factor, foundry casting no longer undercuts the Nauvis recipes.
    - Pentapod eggs got a base value (the biochamber needs eggs, eggs need a biochamber).
    - Biter egg and promethium chunk base values lowered to 165, so 1 red science for 100
      promethium science stays close to the minimum chance.
    - /gamble-values lists hidden items without a value only as a count.
